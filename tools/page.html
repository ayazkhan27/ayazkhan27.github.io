The ceiling that stopped a release

Telenav, Santa Clara. Applied ML and Data Engineering Intern, June to August 2026, on Novo Insurance's scoring stack.

56.69%

A nonparametric estimate of the best accuracy any calibrator could reach on this data: 95% CI [55.13, 58.08] on 4,338 true-ping rows.

  • The production calibrator ran 9 points below that bound. The finding cancelled a release that would have degraded pricing.
  • Caught target leakage and non-temporal splits, which corrected an inflated 0.806 AUC to an honest 0.7401 and surfaced $127K of campaign waste.
  • Privileged-features distillation lifted ping-stage AUC from 0.6206 to 0.6457 on 23,559 holdout rows.
  • A 270M-parameter FunctionGemma router: 95.5% function accuracy and 86.4% exact match on 500 held-out prompts, 95.4% serving accuracy against live Snowflake.

Energy insecurity in Con Edison rate cases

Columbia Data Science Institute scholar, NYSERDA energy insecurity research, January to May 2026.

317 Con Edison testimony PDFs, 16 docket themes. Affordability testimony fell 3.8 points from 2022 to 2025.

The filings came from the New York Public Service Commission. I split them into 12,073 segments, 10,225 of them substantive, and modeled topics with BERTopic: LegalBERT embeddings, UMAP, then HDBSCAN. Over the same years, rate-design testimony rose 4.9 points and IT-systems testimony rose 3.4.
